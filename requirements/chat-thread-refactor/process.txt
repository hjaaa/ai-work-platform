== What Did NOT Work ==

  → 解决：改用 thenAnswer(inv -> 1) 绕过类型推断
- LambdaUpdateWrapper 在纯单元测试中报 "can not find lambda cache"
  → 解决：改用字符串形式的 UpdateWrapper
